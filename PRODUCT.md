# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High-school students (roughly 14–18) building career readiness. They use the app in short daily bursts — between classes, on the bus, at home — on their own phones. The job to be done: complete today's mission, keep the streak alive, climb the leaderboard against classmates.

## Product Purpose

Launch Your Career is a Duolingo-style daily-mission app: one career-readiness challenge question per day, a points/diamonds/streak economy, leaderboards filterable by state/city/school, and a store to redeem points for rewards. Success looks like daily return visits and streaks that survive weekends.

## Brand Personality

Playful, encouraging, energetic. Celebrates wins loudly (confetti, badges), softens losses ("your streak is safe"). Feels like a game first and a learning tool second.

## Anti-references

- Sterile corporate LMS / e-learning portals (gray tables, dry copy).
- Anything that reads as homework or a compliance course.

## Design Principles

1. **One mission per screen** — each screen has a single primary action; everything else recedes.
2. **Celebrate, don't scold** — failure states protect motivation (streak saved, come back tomorrow).
3. **The economy is the interface** — points, diamonds, and streaks are visible everywhere they're earned or spent.
4. **Clay over flat** — soft depth, rounded geometry, physical-feeling buttons (claymorphism on a night-sky backdrop).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text on both the dark night-sky background and light cards.
- Buttons carry accessibility roles/labels (established pattern in BottomTabBar); keep it.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences for celebratory animations where feasible.
